# MEAT SAUCE (TOMATO-BASED)
*Adjusted from Once Upon a Chef*

## Ingredients
* 4 lbs plum tomatoes 
* 6 Tbsp unsalted butter
* 4 Tbsp olive oil
* 2 medium yellow onions, peeled and cut in half
* 4 cloves garlic, peeled and smashed
* 1¼ tsp salt
* 1 tsp sugar
* 1 6-oz. can of tomato paste
* 1 lb ground meat of choice, at least 85% lean
* Fresh basil

## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil. 

2. Fill a large bowl halfway-full with ice cubes and cold water. 

3. Using a sharp knife, cut a ¼-inch deep X on one end of each tomato. 

4. Place the scored tomatoes into the boiling water and cook until you see the skin starting to wrinkle and split, 1 to 2 minutes (be careful not to cook too long, or the tomatoes will become soft and difficult to handle).
   * _4 lbs plum tomatoes_

5. Using a slotted spoon, lift the tomatoes out of the pot and plunge them into the ice-cold water bath. 

6. Let sit for a few minutes to cool, then transfer the tomatoes to a cutting board and, using your hands, peel off their skins. 

7. Cut the tomatoes into ½-inch chunks (discard the cores at this point) and transfer them, along with all of their juices, into a Dutch oven or saucepan.

8. To the chopped tomatoes, add the butter, olive oil, onions, garlic, salt, sugar, and tomato paste. 
   * _6 Tbsp unsalted butter_
   * _4 Tbsp olive oil_
   * _2 medium yellow onions peeled and halved_
   * _4 cloves garlic peeled and smashed_
   * _1 ¼ tsp salt_
   * _1 tsp sugar_
   * _6 oz. can of tomato paste_

9. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce the heat to low and simmer, uncovered, for 1½ to 2½ hours, stirring occasionally and mashing the tomatoes, until the sauce is no longer watery. 

10. Remove and discard the onions. 

11. Just before the sauce has cooked down to desired consistency, heat a large skillet under medium heat and add olive oil. 
    * _1 Tbsp olive oil_

12. Cook your ground meat in the skillet, browning if possible. Drain any water or fat as it separates out. 
    * _1 lb ground meat_

13. As the meat is cooking, return to the sauce pot and use a wooden spoon or potato masher to mash any large chunks of tomatoes and garlic to make a slightly chunky and thick sauce.

14. Once meat is cooked, add it to the sauce pot and stir thoroughly.

15. Taste and adjust seasoning if necessary. 

16. Before serving, stir in the basil.

17. Serve on your pasta of choice.

---

_Tip: If you don't want to use Italian pasta, try Taiwanese knife-cut noodles instead! The sauce sticks to the textured pasta really well. I personally use the Mong Lee Shang brand._
